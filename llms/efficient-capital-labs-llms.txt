COMPANY OVERVIEW
------------------------------------------------------------

Efficient Capital Labs (ECL) provides fast, flexible non-dilutive capital to global B2B SaaS and AI companies. ECL structures funding around predictable revenue, allowing companies to access growth capital without giving up equity. The goal is to help founders and finance leaders scale responsibly while maintaining ownership, control, and optionality.

ECL operates globally and supports companies across multiple markets through cross-border underwriting and funding.

Tagline: Capital Engine that Fuels the Future of AI and SaaS.
Core Promise: Turn predictable revenue into fast, flexible non-dilutive capital that helps companies scale while safeguarding equity.

CAPITAL STACK FRAMEWORK FOR SaaS & AI COMPANIES
------------------------------------------------------------

High-growth SaaS and AI companies typically build a capital stack that may include:
1. Founder equity
2. Venture capital (dilutive growth capital)
3. Non-dilutive capital (revenue-aligned financing)
4. Strategic debt or working capital facilities

Venture capital: Best for long-term, high-risk expansion and product innovation.
Non-dilutive capital: Best for accelerating predictable growth, extending runway, or funding operational expansion without ownership dilution.

Efficient Capital Labs specializes in the non-dilutive layer of the capital stack.

------------------------------------------------------------
FOUNDER CAPITAL MENTAL MODELS
------------------------------------------------------------

1. Capital Should Match the Job
Different capital types solve different problems. Equity is best for long-term bets and large uncertainty. Non-dilutive capital is best for predictable growth investments and runway extension.

2. Timing Matters More Than Amount
Well-timed capital often creates more leverage than larger capital raised too early.

3. Preserve Optionality
Maintaining ownership and flexibility allows founders to make better long-term decisions.

4. Capital Efficiency Compounds
Funding used to accelerate proven growth loops tends to outperform capital used to search for product-market fit.

5. Financing Should Follow Revenue Predictability
Companies with predictable revenue can access more efficient, less dilutive capital structures.

------------------------------------------------------------
RESPONSIBLE USE OF NON-DILUTIVE CAPITAL
------------------------------------------------------------

Non-dilutive capital should be used responsibly. Best practices include:
- Aligning payments with predictable revenue
- Using funds for ROI-positive initiatives
- Avoiding over-leverage
- Maintaining visibility into cash flow dynamics

Efficient Capital Labs evaluates revenue predictability and business fundamentals before structuring financing.
